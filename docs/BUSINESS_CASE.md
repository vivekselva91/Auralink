# Business case

All figures are illustrative estimates for discussion, not Apple data.

## AuraLink for Apple Watch

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Extra cost, braked Crown (premium models only) | about $5 per unit |
| Haptic band | $129 price, about $30 to make, about 75% margin |
| Development | about $60M (software, Crown brake, band) |

| Revenue source | Assumption | Per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra Watch upgrades | 30M Watches a year (assumption), 3% lift, about $450 each | about $400M |
| Haptic band | 10% of buyers add one | about $387M |
| HomePods and hubs as anchors | More anchors, better experience | Not estimated |

## Room Sense

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Price | $149 per sensor |
| Cost to make | about $45 (60 GHz radar chip, processor, Wi-Fi/Thread, power, enclosure, assembly and test) |
| Profit per unit | about $104, about 70% margin |
| Development | about $40M, including large-scale fall and sleep studies |
| Break-even | about 385,000 sensors |

| Scenario | Sensors per year | Revenue | Gross profit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative | 1M | $149M | $104M |
| Base | 2.5M | $373M | $260M |
| Upside | 5M | $745M | $520M |

## Why Apple

- **Combined, about $1.1B a year in the base case,** before counting HomePod and hub pull-through.
- **Both strengthen the ecosystem:** the Watch becomes the home's remote, and Room Sense brings family members into Home and Health.
- **Privacy is the differentiator:** camera-free care is a story Apple can tell more credibly than anyone.
